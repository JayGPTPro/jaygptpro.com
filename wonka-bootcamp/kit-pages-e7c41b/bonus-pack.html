What the pack is, and is not

Every skill reads what the bootcamp already put on disk and produces images, videos or pages you can use. A skill never asks for something status.md already holds, and it never writes over another skill's file.

None of it writes listing copy and none of it gives seller advice. That is what the free amz-* skills are for. The pack is creative only, and it is only for graduates.

Four of them also work outside it. Santa, Seasons, the landing page and the two video skills accept a bare ASIN, a link or a folder of images, for a listing that never went through the line. You lose the locked reference sheet, so the product is only as accurate as the pictures you hand over.

What is in the box

Eleven skills, four jobs.

Each card says what comes out, what you say to Wonka, what it usually costs, and where the files land. Costs are the skills' own estimates. Every skill prices the job before it spends and stops at its cap instead of asking you mid-run.

Not sure where to start? /running-order is free and takes five minutes. /santa shows you the whole idea for about fifteen cents. Leave /listing-autopilot for your next real product.

Install, once

Download, run one line, ask Wonka what is new.

Download wonka-bonus-pack.zip v3 · about 0.6 MB · unpacks to a folder called wonka-bonus-pack
1

Unzip it anywhere

Downloads is fine. You get a folder called wonka-bonus-pack with the eleven skills, the shared engine and the installer inside.

2

Run the installer

Right-click the unzipped folder and choose New Terminal at Folder (Mac) or Open in Terminal (Windows). Type the first two words, then drag your Wonka folder onto the terminal window: it types the path for you.

bash install.sh /path/to/your/Wonka

Your Wonka folder is the one that holds factory/ and CLAUDE.md.

3

Start a NEW session, then ask

Skills are read when a Claude Code session starts. A window that was already open will not see them, however many times you ask. Close it, open your Wonka folder again, then say:

What new skills do I have?

Not a terminal person? Let Wonka install it

Unzip the pack, open your Wonka folder in Claude Code, and paste this. He finds the folder, runs the installer and reports what he installed. Then close the window and open it again, so the new skills are read.

What the installer will and will not do

It copies the eleven skills into .claude/skills/ beside the kit's rooms, and the shared engine into .claude/pack/. It refuses to touch anything that is not a pack skill, so your kit stays exactly as it was. Running it twice is safe.

Wonka's own updates leave the pack alone, and the pack leaves them alone. If you cannot see .claude in Finder, press Cmd + Shift + . On Windows: View → Show → Hidden items.

When it does not go like that

"REFUSED: does not look like a Wonka factory"

The path is wrong. It must be the folder holding factory/ and CLAUDE.md, not the folder above it and not the zip.

Wonka does not know the new skills

The session was already open when you installed. Close that window, open your Wonka folder again, and ask once more.

"rsync: command not found" (Windows)

The installer handles it on its own and copies the files the plain way. If it still stops, use the paste-to-Wonka prompt above and he will copy the folders in.

You cannot see the .claude folder

It is hidden on purpose. Mac: Cmd + Shift + . in Finder. Windows: View, then Show, then Hidden items.

"REFUSED: exists and is not a pack skill"

A folder of that name is already there from something else. Rename it, then install again. Nothing of yours was touched.

A skill stops and talks about a cap

That is the cap doing its job, not a failure. It tells you what it would cost and waits. Raise the cap in the command, or run fewer frames.
