If something goes wrong

Three things that happen, and what they mean.

Wonka does not know the new skills

The window was already open when he installed them. Close it, open Wonka again, and ask once more.

He says the file does not match

He is doing his job: the download was cut short. Ask him to try once more. If it happens twice, message me.

A skill stops and talks about money

That is the spending limit doing its job, not a failure. It tells you what the next step would cost and waits. Tell Wonka a higher limit, or ask for fewer images.

Anything else, message me, same as during the bootcamp. If something a skill made looks wrong, send the file and the one line Wonka said about it.
