What to Give Wonka

ASINs or links, labelled reference photos, or a written description. Specify which parts change and provide accurate measurements or product details where needed. New colors can be explored as concepts.

Three ways to describe the family

  • ASINs or links. One per variation. Wonka reads each child's own listing for its printed details.
  • Labelled reference photos. Name the file by the variation it shows, so the photo and the child cannot be mixed up.
  • A written description. Say which parts change (color, size, shape, count, finish) and give the measurements or product details for each.

Any combination works. Wonka uses the research and approved images already saved in your project and confirms what needs to change before anything runs.

Concepts

A color or finish you do not sell yet can be explored as a labelled concept. It is not a product claim and it does not go on a listing.
