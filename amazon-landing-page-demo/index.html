Can I take it in the pool?

+

Yes. IPX7 means it can be fully immersed up to 3 feet for about 30 minutes. It is rated for the shower, hiking and camping too.

02

How loud is 24 watts?

+

It is two 12 watt drivers with two passive radiators behind them. Buyers describe it as loud enough for a garage, a yard and a car, and compare it to speakers costing three times as much.

03

Does 30 hours mean 30 hours?

+

Ortizan quotes up to 30 hours at around 65 decibels of continuous play or call time. Push the volume higher and it drops, the way it does on any speaker.

04

Can I pair two together?

+

Yes. Buy two and the dual pairing function gives you true left and right surround. Reviewers note the pairing confirms with a chime rather than a message.

05

How far does the Bluetooth reach?

+

The Bluetooth 5.3 chip connects from 66 unobstructed feet, to a phone, a TV or a laptop. There are AUX and TF card inputs as well.

06

Can I turn the lights off?

+

The RGB themes are yours to switch. You can also run the night theme as a plain light with no music playing at all. For anything else, check the listing on Amazon.
