Applicant Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This notice explains how JAY GPT PRO LLC ("I", "me") handles the information you share when you apply to work with me, through the form at jaygptpro.com/hiring, by email, or on WhatsApp.

Who is responsible

JAY GPT PRO LLC, run by Jay Margaliot. Contact: info@jaygptpro.com.

What I collect

  • The details you enter in the form: name, email, WhatsApp number, city and country, LinkedIn, your CV (as a link or an uploaded file), and your answers.
  • Anything you send me directly, such as a CV or portfolio.
  • Notes from our conversations, if you move forward in the process.

Please don't include confidential information about current or past employers or clients.

Why I use it

Only to review your application, contact you about it, and decide whether we work together. I won't add you to my email list or WhatsApp groups because you applied, and I won't use your answers for marketing.

Where it's stored

Applications are saved to a private Google Sheet in the JAY GPT PRO Google Workspace account, uploaded CVs go to a private folder in the same account's Google Drive, and a copy of each submission is emailed to info@jaygptpro.com. Google stores this data on its servers, which may be outside your country, including in the United States. Only I, and people working with me on hiring, can see it.

How AI is used

I use AI tools, including Claude by Anthropic, to help organize and summarize applications. AI does not make hiring decisions. Every decision about who moves forward is made by me. I don't consider your name, nationality, gender, age, religion, or other personal characteristics when assessing your fit.

How long I keep it

I keep applications for up to 12 months after the hiring process closes, then delete them. If you'd like me to delete your application sooner, just ask.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information you've shared at any time by emailing info@jaygptpro.com. Withdrawing your application won't affect anything else you have with JAY GPT PRO.

Changes

If this notice changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
