The role

What the work looks like

I'm building a small, AI powered team and may hire more than one person. The role is intentionally broad, and specific responsibilities will be shaped around each person's strengths and the needs of the business.

These are the main areas of work. They're areas where you could contribute, not seven jobs every person has to do.

Growth & Marketing

Growing our free WhatsApp community and email audience, experimenting with audience acquisition, improving marketing funnels, and helping generate sales.

Content Creation & Copywriting

Researching and preparing valuable AI content for Amazon sellers, writing WhatsApp posts, emails, newsletters, social posts, and launch copy.

Creative Design & Video Production

Creating images, videos, banners, presentations, and marketing assets using AI and other creative tools.

Partnerships & Business Development

Finding collaboration opportunities, researching potential partners, coordinating webinars, managing outreach, and helping expand our reach.

Product Development & Course Launches

Helping turn ideas into new AI Skills, courses, training programs, webinars, and digital products, including launch preparation and execution.

AI Employees & Automation

Building, improving, and managing AI powered workflows and AI Employees to automate recurring business activities.

Project Management & Operations

Taking projects from idea to completion, organizing priorities, coordinating execution, maintaining systems, and making sure things get done.

What this means for you

  • You don't need to be an expert in every area.
  • I'm looking for proactive, versatile people who enjoy taking ownership.
  • People with different strengths are welcome. That's how a good team is built.
  • You won't be expected to own every area of the business. We'll define clear responsibilities based on your strengths, while keeping the flexibility to collaborate across projects.
  • This is a hands on execution role. You'll do the work yourself, using AI, not only plan or manage it.

Who I'm looking for

What matters to me

  • Amazon experience is required. You've worked as a seller, with an Amazon brand, or with sellers as part of an agency or service business.
  • Hands on AI experience. You use AI tools to get real work done and can explain something you've actually built or improved.
  • Ownership and follow through. You can take an unclear idea, ask the right questions, and turn it into a finished result.
  • Good business judgment. You understand the audience, priorities, quality, and what is worth doing first.
  • Curiosity and initiative. You can learn new tools, find answers independently, and bring practical ideas to the table.

Technical skills, automation experience, and familiarity with Claude Code are welcome bonuses, not requirements.

Working together

How we'd work together

You'll work directly with me and have room to take ownership of projects. I'll continue leading the product vision, advanced AI teaching, and key business decisions. I need help with the planning, execution, content, growth, and systems that turn that direction into consistent progress.

We'll agree on priorities and working hours together. This is a full time remote commitment, with meaningful overlap during Israel working hours. Eastern Europe is preferred for practical reasons, but location alone isn't the deciding factor.

I'm not expecting you to know everything on day one. I am expecting initiative, honest communication, a willingness to learn, and the ability to finish what you start.
