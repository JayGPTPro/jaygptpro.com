2

Approve the brief

Claude CodeHub
  • The brief is the plan for the set: what each image has to say and show.
  • Read it in the hub (hit Refresh at the top) or in Claude Code. Approve in either, nothing moves until you do.
  • Never edit the document. Say what you want changed and it rewrites it.
  • Argue with it. "Why did you put that in image 3?" Sometimes the answer is good. Sometimes it says you are right.
Approve the brief
3

Approve the product reference

Claude CodeHub

The reference sheet is the product as the AI understands it.

  • Expect two or three rounds. Multi-part products and thin photos make the first try wrong. That is normal.
  • Say exactly what is off: wrong part, wrong proportion, missing angle, a colour that is not the real one.
  • A good sheet shows several angles, the inside or underside, and something that gives the real size.
Do not wave this one through. If the reference is wrong, every single image after it is wrong, and you will not be able to fix it by editing.
4

Sign off the smoke test

Claude CodeHub

One cheap image per slot, so you can see the brief instead of reading it.

  • You are checking two things only: does the product look like the product, and does each slot say what you wanted.
  • Ignore the design. Not the fonts, not the colours, not the polish. That comes later.
  • This is your last cheap chance to change the brief: "in image 3, drop that message, show the dimensions instead".
Approve the smoke test
This sign-off is the budget gate. After it the big generation runs with no more stops. Look properly before you approve.
5

Pick your images

Genrupt app

The one part you do outside Claude Code, and the longest. Open Genrupt, or follow the link on the run in the hub.

Main images

  • Open them, compare a few side by side, select the ones you like.
  • Have a winner? Mark it Final. Claude reads what you selected and what you marked final.
Genrupt's main images are the weak spot right now. Treat them as ideas, and make the real main image elsewhere if you need to. Everything below is unaffected.

Secondary images

A grid: your slots across, ten style rows down. Up and down is the same image in a different style, left and right is a different slot.

  1. Pick the style first. Move up and down until a row feels right for the product, then click the small style icon on that row. Now the colours and fonts are settled.
  2. Then pick the composition for each slot. Stop looking at colour. Look at what is in the frame: the person, the scene, the angle. Select one per slot.
  3. Restyle and send to Refine. It rebuilds your chosen compositions in your chosen style.
  4. Generate more if you need to. Mobile-friendly on, quality low, four per slot.
  5. Mark the final image per slot. That is what Claude picks up.
Mobile-friendly is not optional. Bigger type, bigger icons, less text. It is what makes an image work on a phone.
Lock the slots you are happy with before you generate again. Otherwise Generate re-rolls every slot that is not final, and you pay for all of them.
6

A+ and the edits

Claude CodeGenrupt appHub
I selected the final images in Genrupt. Continue.
  • It builds the A+ in the same style, then checks your set against the brief and fixes what drifted, on its own.
  • Want something else changed? Name the image and say it: "in slot 5, make the headline the dimensions".
  • Edits are cheap. Ask for as many as you want.
  • A+ edits are better done inside Genrupt. A+ Builder → your project → the edit button, then describe the change, and attach a reference screenshot if you have one.
  • Export from A+ Builder → Selected → zip. It comes out already cut to Amazon's sizes, mobile and desktop.
  • Back in the hub, pick the final set in the cockpit (Compare puts two side by side), Lock selection, then Approve.
7

Read the panel, then you are done

Claude CodeHub
  • Around 80 AI personas, built from the real buyer demographics, react to your locked set before any money is spent.
  • Read the comments, ignore the numbers. The scores are not accurate. The objections are useful.
  • Tell Claude which suggested edits to apply. Then approve.
That closes the run. A full image set and a ready A+, delivered.

Questions

Can I run the whole thing from the hub?No. The work happens in a Claude Code session. The hub is where you see where every product stands, where you approve, and where someone else can pick up your run tomorrow.
Nothing on the run seems to need me.Then it does not. A blue banner means the AI is working. Orange means someone is waiting on you, and the hub bell pings you when that someone is you.
Something is stuck or looks broken.A red "blocked" pill names what is missing. Say it in the team channel. Do not use Override in the "..." menu, it permanently skips a quality gate.
Questions or something that should work better? Send it over and it goes into the pipeline.
