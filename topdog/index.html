The Skill Skiller

Your process in. A working skill out.

It interviews you about the thing you keep doing by hand, asks the questions you would not have thought to answer, then writes the skill, tests it against real cases, and installs it. You describe the job. It builds the tool.

>/skill-skillerI want a skill that writes my weekly supplier update
npx skills add JayGPTPro/the-skill-skiller -g

Listing Audit

Your ASIN in. A full listing audit out.

Pulls your live listing, scores the title, bullets, images, A+ and backend against what actually converts, and writes a branded report you can open in the browser. Findings are ordered by revenue impact, so you fix the expensive one first.

>/amazon-idq-pro-auditB0GYY18GYC
npx skills add JayGPTPro/amazon-idq-pro-audit -g
Needs Bright Data MCP, connected once, to read the live listing

Landing Page

Your ASIN in. A full landing page out.

Reads your listing and builds a complete page: hero, features, your real reviews, FAQ, email capture and a mobile CTA. Then you talk to it. Change this, add that, make the hero warmer. For Meta traffic, for a coupon drop, for anywhere you would rather not send people to a competitor's shelf.

The Santa Skill

Your ASIN in. Your images, Christmas edition.

Takes your secondary images and gives them back as Christmas versions. Same product, same people, same text. It never touches your main image, because Amazon wants that background white. Run it in October and your gallery is ready for the quarter that carries the year.

>/santa-skillB0GYY18GYC
npx skills add JayGPTPro/santa-skill -g
Needs an OpenAI API key. Single digit cents per image See the before and afters →

ASIN to Video Autopilot

Your ASIN in. A finished product video out.

A full thirty second film, decided and assembled without asking you anything: an emotional hook, real use cases with people, one signature camera move, sound that belongs to the scene, music, voiceover and animated text. You set a cost cap once. It stays under it.
