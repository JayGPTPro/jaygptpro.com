4

Make your first film

Pick who gives the gift and who gets it: Mom or Dad, to a son, a daughter, a husband or a wife. Then type this in Claude, with your own product's Amazon link and your family:

Claude reads your listing, asks you one question about the price, and makes the film. It tells you the credits before every step that uses them. About half an hour later Claude opens a folder with two files: the full film, about a minute, and a 30-second cut for ads.

Want another family later? Just tell Claude, for example: "Make it Dad giving to our daughter." Several products? Paste all the links in one message.

Questions

Which file do I upload to Amazon?

The full film (the file that ends in your ASIN) goes on your listing. The file that ends in "_30s" is the short cut, made for ads like Sponsored Brands video. Both are full HD.

Does it work for every product?

It works for a product that makes a good gift and is opened and used at home. If a product is not a good fit, Claude tells you before it spends any credits.

What does Claude ask me about the people in the film?

The people in the film are AI actors, made for this film. Genrupt asks you once to confirm that before it makes your first clip. Claude asks you, and confirms it for you when you say yes.

How do I get updates?

When we improve the film, tell Claude: "Update my Perfect Gift Kit." Your films and settings stay as they are.

Can I make films for other sellers?

The kit is for your own products. Making films for other sellers or reselling them is not included.

I'm stuck.

First tell Claude exactly what you see; it usually knows the fix. If not, write to info@jaygptpro.com and include your kit code.
