Set Up Your Kit

Set up Genrupt, connect it to Claude, and install your kit. We'll go through each step together. You only do this once.

This video is on its way.

Before you start

  • Claude Code with a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher).
  • A Genrupt account with video credits. You buy the credits from Genrupt, separately from this kit. You'll set this up below.
  • About 3 GB of free space on your computer. The kit takes about 1 GB, and each film adds about half a GB.

No API keys needed.

1. Set up Genrupt

Genrupt makes the new video clips for your films. Three steps:

1
Sign up. Create your Genrupt account at genrupt.com/l/aivault.
2
Get credits. Choose a monthly plan or buy credits, whichever suits you. On your first purchase, use the code JAY20 to get 20% extra credits (first purchase only). A film uses a few dozen credits.
3
Connect it to Claude. In the Claude app, click Plugins in the side menu, then Add, Add custom connector. Name it Genrupt, paste this address, click Continue, and sign in to Genrupt.
https://genrupt.com/api/agent/mcp

Genrupt now shows under Plugins, Your plugins. No Plugins in your side menu? Click + next to the message box and look for Connectors there. Stuck? Go on anyway: Claude walks you through the connection.

Already using Higgsfield?

You can use it with this kit too. Add it the same way, with this address:

https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp

The lessons show Genrupt, which we recommend: it cleans up your product photos before every clip. If both are connected, Claude uses Genrupt.

Create Your First Film

Choose a family, add your Amazon product link, and send the message to Claude.

This video is on its way.

1. Your Amazon product link

Your product page on any Amazon store (amazon.com, .co.uk, .de, .ca...), a short a.co link, or just its ASIN for Amazon.com (like B0XXXXXXXX).

2. Choose a family

Choose one of the eight ready-made families: who gives the gift, and who opens it on Christmas morning.

The toddler films need a product a 3-year-old can really use. If yours isn't, Claude tells you and suggests another family.

Want someone else in the film?

Grandma, Grandpa, a 6-year-old, a teenager: tell Claude who gets the gift (Mom or Dad gives it). Claude creates the new person to look like part of the film's family, shows you a few options to pick from, and makes that person's scenes again.

It costs more than a ready-made family, because every scene with the new person is made again: about 250 Genrupt credits (around $12 to $15), once. Claude tells you the cost before it starts. After that, every film with this family costs the same as a ready-made one.

3. Your message to Claude

Paste your product link and choose a family. Your message appears here.

Paste it into Claude Code, the same way you pasted the setup message.

What happens next

  • Claude reads your listing and checks that the product fits the film before it spends any credits. If Amazon won't let Claude read the page, Claude asks you to save it from Chrome (File, Save Page As) and tells you exactly what to do.
  • It makes the new video clips on your Genrupt account, checks the product in them against your listing photos, and puts the film together.
  • Before every step that uses credits, Claude tells you the cost.
  • When the film is ready, Claude opens the folder with your two videos.

A film uses a few dozen Genrupt credits, about $2 to $4 depending on your plan, and more if a clip needs another try or you ask for changes. In our test, the first film took under 15 minutes. If a clip doesn't match your product, Claude makes it again, up to three tries, and asks you before going past that.

Before you upload the film anywhere, watch it and make sure your product looks right. Lesson 4 shows what to check.

Edit and Use Your Films

Check your film, fix what needs fixing, make more films, and use your videos.

This video is on its way.

Check your film

Before you upload it, check these three things:

  1. The phone shows the right product photo and name.
  2. The product that rises out of the phone is complete, with all its parts.
  3. In the gift scene, it's your product, and it's used the right way.

Claude checks the clips too, but AI can still make mistakes. You're the final quality check.

Make changes

Tell Claude what you'd like to fix, in plain words. It works on that part of the film and builds the film again. If new clips are needed, Claude shows you the credit cost first.

Change the text on the phoneNo video credits
Make the product title on the phone shorter.
Change the product photoNo video credits
Use a different photo of my product on the phone. Show me the options from my listing.
Fix the gift sceneA new video clip (uses credits)

Write what you see in your own words. For example:

In the gift scene, the headphones are missing the left ear cup. Fix it.
Change the familyA new video clip (uses credits)

Name the family you want. For example:

Make the same film again with Dad giving it to his daughter.

Make more films

Send Claude a new Amazon product link and choose the family, like in Lesson 3. You don't install anything again.

Want Christmas films for several products? Send all the product links in one message. Claude makes a film for each one, with the same family for all or a different one per product, and shows you the estimated total credit cost before it starts.

For example, seven products in one message (with your own links):

Make Christmas films for these products. Mom gives it to her son. https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0XXXXXXX1 https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0XXXXXXX2 https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0XXXXXXX3 https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0XXXXXXX4 https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0XXXXXXX5 https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0XXXXXXX6 https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0XXXXXXX7

Where to use your films

  • The full film (perfect_gift_B0XXXXXXXX.mp4) is made for your Amazon product page.
  • The 30-second version (the file that ends in _30s) is made for ads, like Sponsored Brands video.
  • Both work on your Brand Store, your website and social media.

Amazon reviews videos before they go live, and approval isn't guaranteed. Check the requirements of the placement you use.

Christmasify Your Images

The Santa Skill turns your listing's lifestyle photos into Christmas versions. Type one ASIN or paste your whole catalog. The product stays exactly as it is, and the main image is never touched.

This video is on its way.

Get the Santa Skill

It runs in Claude Code or in Codex. Pick the one you use. Here is what each one needs.

Claude Code

Draws with your OpenAI key.
  • Claude Code, the desktop app or the terminal.
  • An OpenAI API key on a verified organization. The skill draws with gpt-image-2, and a verified organization unlocks it. Never connected OpenAI to Claude Code? This guide walks you through it.
  • Python 3 with the openai and Pillow packages. The first run prints the exact install line if anything is missing.
~9¢ an image, on your OpenAI account.

Codex

Draws with your ChatGPT plan. No API key.
  • Codex, signed in with your ChatGPT account.
  • A ChatGPT plan. The images come out of your plan's usage limits, so a big catalog may take more than one session.
  • Python 3 with Pillow and opencv-python-headless. The first run checks for them.
  • Node, for the one-line install.
$0 extra: it runs inside your ChatGPT plan.

Install it once

Claude Code

1
Download the skill.
Download santa-skill.zip
2
Paste this into Claude Code. Claude asks before it writes to that folder: click Allow. Then open a new session.
Install the santa-skill skill from my Downloads: unzip santa-skill.zip (or take the santa-skill folder, if it is already unzipped) into ~/.claude/skills, so that ~/.claude/skills/santa-skill/SKILL.md exists.

Have Node? This one line installs it instead:

npx skills add JayGPTPro/santa-skill -g

Codex

Paste this into your terminal:

npx skills add JayGPTPro/santa-skill-codex -g -a codex

Run it, for every listing

In Claude Code:

/santa-skill [ASIN]

In Codex:

$santa-skill [ASIN]

Replace [ASIN] with yours, or paste a whole list of product links. It fetches your images, decides which ones to change and which to skip, and runs them with no questions.

What you get

  • A christmas folder with the new images, about 1024 px. Amazon accepts 1000 px and up; for a crisp zoom, upscale to 1600 or more before you upload.
  • A before and after page with a one-line verdict for each image, and one page for the whole catalog.
  • Your originals stay as they are, so you swap the Christmas images in for Q4 and back out in January.

What it never does

  • It never touches the main image. Amazon wants it on pure white.
  • It never changes the product: same shape, color, angle, label and screen.
  • It never changes the people. Faces, poses and hands stay as shot. Only a summer scene gets winter clothes.
  • One Santa hat at most, on one adult, never on a child or the product. Snow only where snow belongs.
Questions and Help

Questions

Find answers to common questions, or contact me for help.

What do I need to use the kit?

Claude Code with a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher), a Genrupt account with credits, and about 3 GB of free space on your computer. No API keys needed.

How much does it cost to make a film?

A few dozen Genrupt credits, about $2 to $4 depending on your plan. A clip that needs another try, or a change you ask for, uses more. Claude tells you the cost before every step that uses credits.

How long does it take?

Our test took under 15 minutes, from the first message to the finished film. Times vary with the product and with how long Genrupt takes to make the clips.

Where are my finished videos?

Claude opens the folder when your film is ready. To find it later, tell Claude "Open my film folder." On your computer it's in the PerfectGift folder, under engine, jobs: one folder for each product and family, with the videos inside its "out" folder.

What if my product doesn't look right?

Tell Claude exactly what's wrong, for example a missing part, the wrong color, or the product used the wrong way. Claude fixes that part of the film. A new clip uses credits, and Claude tells you the cost first.

Can I make another film?

Yes. You can make films for as many of your own products as you like. Each new film uses video credits, and Claude shows you the estimated cost before it starts. You don't install the kit again.

Can I have a different family, like grandparents or a teenager?

Yes, for the person who gets the gift (Mom or Dad gives it). Tell Claude who: Grandma, a teenager, a 6-year-old. Claude creates the new person, lets you pick from a few options, and makes their scenes again. That costs about 250 credits once, and Claude shows you the cost first. After that the family is saved for every film.

Does it work for every product?

The kit works best with products that make good gifts and can be shown naturally at home. Claude checks for possible problems before it makes the film, but you still need to watch the final result.

Can I use the kit with Higgsfield?

Yes. If you already have Higgsfield, connect it the same way as Genrupt (the address is in Lesson 2). The lessons show Genrupt, which we recommend because it cleans up your product photos before every clip.

How do I update the kit?

Tell Claude: "Update my Perfect Gift Kit." Your films and settings stay as they are.

Can I make films for other sellers?

The kit is licensed for your own products. Making films for other sellers or reselling the kit isn't included.

Need help?

Start by telling Claude what happened and what you expected. It usually knows the fix. Still stuck? Message me with a short description and, if you can, a screenshot.
