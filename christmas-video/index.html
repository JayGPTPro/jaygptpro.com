09Questions

Before you order

Is it really my product in the film?

Yes. We build from your real product photos and check every scene against them before you get the film.

What do I need to send?

The link to your product on Amazon, and who gives and receives the gift. You fill that in on one short card per film, right after you pay or later from the link in your email. The photos come from your listing. If we need anything else, we ask.

Does it work for my product?

It works best for a product that makes a good gift and fits in a gift box. Bigger than that, or not sure? Write to info@jaygptpro.com before you order.

Can I use my own family in the film?

Not in this version. Every film uses the same cast of actors. You pick the roles: Mom or Dad gives, and a son, a daughter, or a wife or husband receives. The product is yours. A gift from a grandparent, a sibling or a friend is not one of the options yet.

How old is the child in the film?

When a son or daughter receives the gift, the child in the film is about 8 to 12 years old. If your product is for a much younger child or a teenager, write to us at info@jaygptpro.com before you order and we will tell you what we can do.

How fast do I get it?

Within 48 hours of your order, counted in business days.

Can I use it in Amazon ads?

Yes. It is widescreen Full HD, the format for Sponsored Brands video ads, and it is yours to use on your store page and on social too. Amazon reviews new video ads before they run, so order early.

What if something needs to change?

One round of changes is included with every film.

Is it made with AI?

Yes. AI video tools build it, and we check every scene by hand. That is how a full Christmas story is ready in 48 hours.

What is in "Make them yourself"?

The Claude Skill that puts a product into the film, and step-by-step instructions for running it. It arrives by email within 48 hours of payment. You pay the tools directly for what you use, such as your Claude subscription and the video generation credits.

Why is it half price now?

It is the launch price for the first 30 customers. After that the price goes back to $199 per film, and the Skill to $599.
