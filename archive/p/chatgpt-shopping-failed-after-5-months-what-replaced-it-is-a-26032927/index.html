ChatGPT Shopping failed after 5 months. What replaced it is actually more relevant for...

OpenAI tried to build Amazon inside ChatGPT. In September 2025 they launched Instant Checkout. Ask ChatGPT "what headphones should I buy?", see products, and pay right there in the chat. No external site needed.

It flopped. Users searched for products in the chat but didn't actually buy there. Inventory sync issues, tax headaches, and fraud prevention gaps made it worse. Few merchants joined, and on March 24 OpenAI made it official: Instant Checkout is dead.

So what now?

ChatGPT became a product discovery engine. You describe what you want, see products visually with prices and reviews, compare side by side, and even upload a photo to find similar items. When you're ready to buy, it sends you to the merchant's own site.

Who's already in? Walmart (with a full app inside ChatGPT), Target, Sephora, Nordstrom, Best Buy, Home Depot, Wayfair.

On Shopify? You're in automatically. Shopify connected their Catalog directly to ChatGPT. No signup, no integration needed.

Other platforms? Apply at chatgpt.com/merchants. (currently available in the US).

Two things worth doing right now:

1. Open ChatGPT and ask it to recommend products in your category. See what comes up. Which competitors appear? What does ChatGPT say about them? About your product? Most sellers have never checked this. It's like Googling yourself, but on a channel most people are still ignoring.

2. Check your Google Analytics for traffic from chatgpt.com. OpenAI is already sending buyers to merchant sites. If you have traffic from there, you're already part of the game and didn't know it.
