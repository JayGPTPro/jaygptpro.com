It is no longer "almost." It is the real deal

Seedance 2.0 just dropped, the new AI video model from ByteDance (the company behind TikTok and CapCut), and it is shaking the entire industry. The line between AI and reality has officially been erased.

To understand the level we are talking about, watch the attached video by The Dor Brothers. It went viral today after being created in just 24 hours. Try to tell it is AI. You cannot.

I have spent years following AI video and it always felt like we were "almost" there. Impressive but still distinguishable. That gap has officially closed.

Here is what makes Seedance 2.0 different:

🎬 Director level control. No more guessing. You can input 12 reference files simultaneously (including 9 images, 3 videos, and audio) to lock characters, motion, and style.

🔊 Native visual sound. The model generates video and audio together. Perfect lip sync and environmental sound like footsteps or breaking glass, synced precisely to the frame.

💎 True 2K resolution. The quality speaks for itself.

The model is currently only open in China, but the global launch is expected on February 24th.

2026 was always going to be the year of AI video. But if this is the level in February, I truly cannot imagine where we will be by the end of the year. 🔥
