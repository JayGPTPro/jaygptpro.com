Important update for anyone running ads or selling online with AI

A new New York law kicks in today, June 9. It’s a small detail, but it’s going to affect almost everyone running ads or product listings.

Here is the bottom line:
If your ad (or even your Amazon listing image) features an AI-generated person (an AI model, influencer, or fake UGC creator), the law now requires a small label stating that the person isn't real.

Let’s clear up a few things:
✅ You can absolutely keep using AI. The law doesn't ban it at all.
✅ AI backgrounds, product shots, and AI copy are perfectly fine. No disclosure needed.
✅ It’s less dramatic than it sounds. From what's reported, this involves civil penalties, not the kind of mass private lawsuits that spark a wave of consumer claims.
⚠️ That said: Penalties can reach $5,000, and it applies no matter where your business is located, as long as a user in NY sees the content.

What’s the play here?
Just add a small, readable text like "AI-generated" directly on the creative itself (don't bury it in the caption). Build this into your workflow with your agencies or freelancers, and you won't have to overthink it again.
