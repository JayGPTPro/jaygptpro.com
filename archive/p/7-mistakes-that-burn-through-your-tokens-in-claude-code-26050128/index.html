7 Mistakes That Burn Through Your Tokens in Claude Code

1. Mistake: Bloating your CLAUDE.md file.
✅ The Fix: This file loads every session and stays there until the end, even when it is irrelevant. Keep it under 200 lines (only basics and common commands). Move everything else to Skills that load only when you really need them.

2. Mistake: Jumping straight into work without planning.
✅ The Fix: Always start in Plan Mode. Plan what you want to do, catch mistakes early, and only when you have a clear plan, move on to actually building it.

3. Mistake: Using the Opus model for every little task.
✅ The Fix: Opus is brilliant but incredibly wasteful. The winning formula: Haiku for quick tasks, Sonnet for writing and daily work, and Opus strictly for complex planning.

4. Mistake: Leaving Extended Thinking on maximum.
✅ The Fix: "Thinking" tokens are billed as output and cost a lot. For easy tasks (fixing a small typo or a simple change), lower the settings to Medium or Low. Save High for real, complex tasks.

5. Mistake: Telling Claude it made a mistake in a new message.
✅ The Fix: If you got a result you did not like, do not write "this is not what I wanted" and add unnecessary history to the chat. Instead, click the rewind to here edit button on the relevant message and rephrase from there. This way you replace the history instead of letting it pile up.

6. Mistake: Moving to a new task but staying in the same chat.
✅ The Fix : Use /clear between unrelated tasks. Every message resends the entire chat history. There is no reason a conversation about topic A should keep costing you tokens when you are now working on topic B.

7. Mistake: Dragging out long conversations forever.
✅ The Fix: Claude remembers the entire context and it kills your quota. Every 15 to 20 messages, export the chat and start a brand new conversation using the previous one as a reference.

Once you work smart and avoid these mistakes, you will see your tokens last for way more work! 🚀
