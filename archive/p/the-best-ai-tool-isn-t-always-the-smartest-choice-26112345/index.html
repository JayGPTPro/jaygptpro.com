The Best AI Tool Isn't Always the Smartest Choice

Lately, I've been hearing from more and more sellers who are frustrated, wondering if they need to switch platforms again just to keep up with the best tool on the market.

First, everyone was talking about Claude Code. Then Codex. Now it's Grokbot 🤯

If this sounds familiar, here's how I see it.

I personally get excited about every new AI tool that comes out 😅 I love experimenting with them! That's why I keep sharing new tools and updates with you. The goal is to explore what's possible, not to rebuild your entire business every time something new comes out.

And yes, sometimes a new tool really is better. Grokbot, for example, takes things to another level when it comes to proactively handling tasks and working in the background.

But there's a big difference between trying a new tool and moving your entire business to it.

Especially if you've already spent months building workflows, connecting systems, and training your team. Just because a new tool is better doesn't automatically mean switching is worth it.

Here's something worth remembering: most AI platforms eventually catch up with each other. We've seen it happen again and again, and we can already see that direction with Claude Duet and ChatGPT Dots.

So sometimes it's smarter to keep an eye on the new capabilities coming to the tools you already use, rather than constantly chasing whichever platform happens to be the best right now.

Of course, there's no right or wrong here. It's all about what works best for you, your style, and your business.
