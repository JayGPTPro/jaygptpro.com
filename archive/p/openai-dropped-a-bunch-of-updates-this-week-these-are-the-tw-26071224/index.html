OpenAI dropped a bunch of updates this week. These are the two I think are actually...

I summarized everything you need to know in 2 minutes.

1. GPT-5.6

The new model family comes in three versions:
* Sol. The strongest. For complex tasks.
* Terra. Balanced. For most everyday work.
* Luna. Fast and cheap. For short, simple tasks.

2. ChatGPT Work

This is no longer just another chatbot.

Work is a new agent-style mode. Instead of answering one message, it takes a goal, breaks it into steps, works through them, and comes back with a finished result.

For example:
* Reads your email and calendar and builds your plan for the day.
* Analyzes a whole folder of files, like supplier docs or review exports, and returns one summary.
* Writes documents, slide decks, or spreadsheets.
* Stops before any critical action, like sending an email or making a change.

How do you access it?
If you got the update, you’ll see a Chat | Work toggle at the top. If it is not showing yet, it is rolling out gradually.

⚠️ The catch: Work runs on your Codex quota, not your regular chat quota. Long tasks burn through it fast, so test it on one real task before you hand it a full week of work.

👀 My take after a few tests:
Sol is still not at Fable 5’s level, but it gives Opus 4.8 a real fight.

If you already use Claude Code or Codex, most of this will feel familiar.

The real news is that OpenAI is starting to bring these capabilities to ChatGPT users, in an interface almost anyone can use.
