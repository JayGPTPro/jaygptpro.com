In December, Sell to the Giver, Not the User

Christmas shopping has started, and a lot of your buyers aren't buying for themselves anymore. They're buying for someone else.

That changes everything. A gift buyer doesn't care about "20 oz, double-wall insulation, BPA free." They're asking: Will they love it? Will it get there in one piece?

The good news? Your reviews already answer that, in your customers' own words.

👉 Paste your reviews and your current bullets into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, and add this:

"Find every review where this product was bought as a gift. Tell me who it was for, the words buyers used about the reaction, and what they worried about. Then rewrite my bullets for gift buyers using their words, and answer their worries. Show old and new side by side. Use only what's in the reviews, no direct quotes."

We tested it on a 20 oz tumbler. Two worries came up again and again:
💧 Will it leak in their bag?
📦 Will it arrive damaged?
The listing answered neither. That's how gift sales slip away.

One thing to watch: use their words, not their reviews. Amazon doesn't allow customer quotes in your listing.

Let your reviews write your gift copy.
