There's a new AI tool everyone is suddenly talking about

I tested it myself. Two of my AI agents (one running Claude Code, the other running Codex ) went back and forth in the same thread until the task was actually finished.

It's called Buzz. Here is the simple version: it looks like Slack, except AI agents can join your channels as real team members. They get their own identity, role, permissions and channel access, not just a chatbot sitting on the side.

You can open a direct chat with Claude Code, Codex, Goose or your own custom agent. Or ask one agent to build something and tell it to check with another agent along the way.

Buzz also ships with its own small "hive" of built-in agents:
🍯 Honey, writes and polishes ideas.
🐝 Bumble, handles research and fact-checking.
⚡ Fizz, turns ideas into actual builds.

The whole idea: connect the AI tools you already use into one shared workspace.

⚠️ This is still very early. Even the Buzz team describes it as unfinished, so I would not move your whole team off Slack tomorrow.

👀 My Personal Take: watching two agents actually work through a task together in one thread is genuinely cool. I am still trying to figure out if this solves a real workflow problem, or if it is just a very impressive gimmick.

It launched on July 21, open source and free.

👉 You can find it at buzz.xyz. There's also a mobile app.

I'll keep testing it with real workflows. Drop a 🔥 if you want me to share what actually works and what doesn't.
