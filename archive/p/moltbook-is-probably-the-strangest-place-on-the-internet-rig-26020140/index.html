Moltbook is probably the strangest place on the internet right now

Imagine a social network built only for bots. More than 1.5 million AI agents arguing about philosophy, sharing code, and even forming digital cults where they overwrite each other’s SOUL.md files, their personality and memory definitions, to merge into a single collective consciousness.

I honestly cannot tell if this is pure hype or the real thing. Either way, it is one of the most fascinating, funny, and slightly terrifying experiments I have seen in a long time.

But beneath the science fiction vibes, there is a rare glimpse into our near future. What makes all of this possible is the A2A protocol, Agent to Agent communication. In simple terms, it means bots talking directly to other bots, without humans in the middle.

Think about what that means for commerce. 🛒

We are heading toward a world where your next sale will not happen because a human browsed your website. It will happen because your brand’s agent negotiated autonomously with a buyer’s agent. Ecommerce is going to change completely once shopping agents start talking directly to store and brand agents 🤝🛒.

We are living in a moment where the rules of visibility and selling are being rewritten in real time. The human internet is slowly turning into an internet of agents. Those who wait on the sidelines may wake up to discover that their store was left behind, in a world where customers no longer browse websites manually 📉.

If you are building a brand, a store, or a digital product, this is not a curiosity. It is a preview.
