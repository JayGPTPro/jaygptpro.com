I'm loving Tab! A FREE AI assistant on WhatsApp that even makes phone calls for me

Meet Tab, an AI agent you can message on WhatsApp like any other contact. It has its own computer, browser, and phone number, so it can get things done for you.
You can send it voice notes or simply forward a WhatsApp message and ask it to handle it. That's what makes having an AI agent right inside WhatsApp so convenient.

I've been using it quite a lot lately, and my favorite feature is definitely the phone calls.
I've had Tab call an airline to make a small change to my flight reservation and handle another booking when I needed to change dates.

Think about those annoying calls you keep putting off. Checking with a supplier about a late shipment, chasing a carrier about a stuck package, or dealing with customer service. Now you can just send a WhatsApp message and let Tab make the call.

It also researches online, makes bookings, fills out forms, works with files, and connects to your email and calendar.
I even sent it an Amazon product link and asked it to create a video using the product images. It delivered the finished video right in our WhatsApp chat!

💡 Bonus tip for advanced users: This is where things get really interesting.

If you've connected WhatsApp to Claude, you can give your AI employee the ability to make phone calls!
That's exactly what I did with Donna, my personal AI assistant built with Claude Code.

Whenever Donna needs something done over the phone, she prepares all the relevant context and sends Tab the instructions through WhatsApp. Tab then makes the call for her.
My Claude AI employee can now pick up the phone and get things done in the real world.

Tab is similar to Instinct, which I recommended here a few weeks ago. I still use both. What I especially love about Tab is the phone calls, but the downside is that it's relatively slow, and sometimes there's a noticeable delay in its responses.
