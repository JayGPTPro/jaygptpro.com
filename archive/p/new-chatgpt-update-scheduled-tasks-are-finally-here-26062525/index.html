New ChatGPT Update: Scheduled Tasks are finally here

Now, you can define in advance what you want ChatGPT to do and when you want it done.

Instead of using it only when you remember to open ChatGPT and ask for something, you can turn it into an assistant that works on a regular schedule.

For example:

📌 Every morning at 8:00 AM
Send me a short daily briefing with the most important updates that could affect my business today.

📌 Every Monday morning
Give me a few content and marketing ideas I can use this week for my products.

📌 Once a week
Check if there are any important updates, new tools, or changes sellers should know about, and only send me the things that are actually worth paying attention to.

And the best part is that you can set it up from the regular ChatGPT chat.

You can simply write something like:

"Create a recurring task that sends me a short ecommerce briefing every morning at 8:00 AM with the most important things I should know today."

In some interfaces, you may also be able to click `/` and choose Scheduled Task, if it appears for you.

You can create one-time tasks, recurring daily or weekly tasks, reminders, briefings, topic monitoring, and updates based on your interests.

In my opinion, this is one of the most practical updates ChatGPT has released.

Because once scheduling is built in, ChatGPT is no longer just something that answers you when you reach out.

It can start working for you at set times, based on what you defined in advance.

Bottom line:

If you use this correctly, it can save you time, reduce mental load, and help you avoid checking the same things over and over again.
