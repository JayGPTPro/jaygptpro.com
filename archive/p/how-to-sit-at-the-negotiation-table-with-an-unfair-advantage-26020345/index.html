How to Sit at the Negotiation Table with an Unfair Advantage

Most sellers prepare for a negotiation like it is a presentation. They write bullet points and hope for the best.

But here is a completely different angle you might not have considered: Using AI as a mental training simulator.

Before you sit at the negotiation table with a supplier, 3PL, or agency, use Advanced Voice Mode as your "dry run" sparring partner.

Turn the AI into your opponent and let it push you to the limit before the real call.

How to do it:
1️⃣ Open ChatGPT and tap the sound wave icon. 2️⃣ Define the scenario. Here is a strong example:
"I am about to negotiate with a supplier. Act as a stubborn factory owner. Raw material costs are up. I will ask for a 5% discount. Your goal: Give me hard objections (labor costs, exchange rates) and do not give in easily. Let’s argue."

Why this works like magic:
* Real-time response: The new voice model hears your intonation (hesitation or confidence), responds without delay, and allows you to interrupt it mid-sentence. It feels just like a real phone call.
* Immunity to surprises: You face every objection before the meeting, so you have answers ready immediately.
* Confidence: You arrive at the negotiation table calm, focused, and ready to win.

This is the shift from using AI as a "Personal Assistant" to using AI as an "Opponent Coach."
