Gems vs GPTs: Where Should You Build Your AI Team?

Building a team of AI Agents is the smartest move you can make today to streamline your work.

But right now, we have two main options: OpenAI’s GPTs and Google’s Gems. They might look similar, but under the hood they work completely differently.

Before you start building, here is a quick comparison to save you time and frustration:

🧠 1. Memory. Here Gems take the lead. They have a massive context window (up to 2 million tokens). You can feed them entire projects and thick books, and they remember everything. With GPTs, it is easier to hit information overload and have them forget details during long conversations.

⚡️ 2. Live Files (Real Time Data). Gems are connected directly to your Google Drive. Did you update a document, price list, or SOP? The Agent sees it immediately. With GPTs, the information is usually static. If you change a file on your computer, you will need to reupload it manually.

👀 3. The Eyes (Video Analysis). Gems are champions at analyzing content. They can watch YouTube videos or uploaded files and extract insights in seconds. With GPTs, this process is much more cumbersome and often requires external transcription.

🔗 4. Sharing & Distribution (ChatGPT’s Ace). This is the huge advantage of GPTs. They were built as apps that you can easily share via a link with your team, clients, or your community. Gems are designed more as an internal, secure tool within your own Workspace.
