AI tips from the group · October 1, 2026 · AI news or launch

OpenAI announced more than 20 new updates in a single day this week

Here are the 5 announcements that caught my attention the most:

1️⃣ Dots: OpenAI's new AI workers
This was probably the most interesting announcement.
Until now, most AI has worked like this: you ask for something, it gives you an answer, and you move on.

With Dots, the idea is different:
Don't just give AI a task. Give it responsibility.

Each Dot gets its own computer and browser in the cloud. It can connect to your tools, remember its work, and keep working even when you're away from your computer.
Instead of repeatedly asking it to check emails, invoices, or tasks, that can simply become its area of responsibility. It knows what's happening, identifies what needs attention, and acts within the permissions you've given it.
OpenAI even introduced Special Dots that aren't tied to a specific person, but to a role within an organization. We're starting to talk about digital workers that can become permanent parts of a company.

Sound familiar? 😅

2️⃣ ChatGPT Space: You, your team, and your AI workers in the same place
OpenAI introduced a new workspace where people and AI agents can work together across projects, documents, and files.
In the demo, they could tag an agent inside a document, assign it a task, and watch it get to work.
Instead of AI living in a separate window, it becomes part of the workspace itself.

It's a pretty crazy glimpse into what office work could look like in the near future.

3️⃣ You'll be able to build and distribute apps inside ChatGPT
OpenAI is expanding ChatGPT from a platform we use into a platform we can build on.
Developers will be able to create Plugins and apps that work directly inside ChatGPT and distribute them through the Plugin Directory.
If this works the way they're planning, ChatGPT could become a massive new distribution channel for software.

Think App Store for the AI era.

4️⃣ GPT-6.1 Sol
Yep, just one week after GPT-6 😅
According to OpenAI, the new model gets close to Astra in some capabilities, including coding and Computer Use, while costing significantly less through the API.
OpenAI also introduced Ultrafast, allowing agents to operate much faster.

It might sound less exciting than Dots, but when agents are performing dozens of actions for you, speed and cost become critical.

5️⃣ Codex moves to the cloud
You can give Codex a task, close your computer, and it keeps working.
You can also manage multiple agents in parallel and continue controlling the work from the web or your phone.

Again, the same direction: Less AI waiting for us. More AI working for us.

And that's the bigger story behind all of these announcements.
Dots, Space, cloud-based Codex, Plugins, and faster, cheaper models aren't 5 separate stories.

They're all pointing in the same direction: AI that doesn't just answer questions, but takes responsibility, connects to our tools, and does real work over time.

That's exactly the direction I've been talking about for a while with AI Employees. The gap between "using AI" and "employing AI" is closing very quickly.
And I think one of the most important skills over the next few years will be less about knowing how to do every task ourselves, and more about knowing how to build, train, and manage the AI employees that do it for us.
