AI doesn't just make things up. It makes things up with absolute confidence

Steven Schwartz, a lawyer in New York, asked ChatGPT to find precedents for a case he was working on. He got six cases back with names, legal quotes, and detailed analyses. They all looked completely real.

None of them existed.

When he asked the chatbot if they were real, it insisted they were. The final court fine was $5,000.

That is being confidently wrong at the highest level. 😄

Here is the trap: AI sounds exactly as confident when it is guessing as when it knows the facts. You cannot tell where the truth ends and the guessing begins.

If ChatGPT can invent a whole court case, it can easily invent the material, dimensions, or certifications in your product bullet points. One wrong spec is all it takes to get your listing suppressed or your account flagged.

So do not let it fool you.

Most people try to check the facts after the text is written. It is much better to lock the facts before it starts writing.

Paste this prompt before you ask it to write your listing:

"Write the text using only the facts I provided. Do not invent any materials, dimensions, certifications, or specifications. If any information is missing, write [missing info] and ask me. Never present a guess as a fact."

You are not asking it to be less creative in your copywriting. You are just telling it exactly where it is not allowed to invent.

Leave a 👍 if AI has already confidently lied to you.
