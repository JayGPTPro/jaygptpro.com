AI tips from the group · August 13, 2026 · Claude

I’ve noticed that most people using Claude keep it on the same model and the same...

And honestly, it is confusing.

So let’s try to simplify this.

The model = what Claude is capable of.
The effort = how hard Claude works on the problem.

If Claude understands the task, but the answer is too shallow or rushed:
Raise the effort.

If Claude is genuinely hitting a wall and the task needs stronger capabilities:
Change the model.

A useful analogy Anthropic shared:
Sonnet at high effort = an afternoon with a strong generalist
Opus at low effort = five minutes with an expert
Fable = a specialist who can spot things others miss

Why does this matter? Better results, less wasted usage.
