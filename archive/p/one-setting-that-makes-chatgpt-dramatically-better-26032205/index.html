One setting that makes ChatGPT dramatically better

If you use ChatGPT's Thinking mode, you already know it gives better answers than Auto. But there's a setting inside it that takes it much further: Extended Thinking

💡 This is where the model gets permission to really go deep. Cross reference data, reason through complex problems, take its time before answering.

For example, next time you upload a spreadsheet with 500 product reviews and ask "find patterns in the negative feedback and suggest product improvements," try it in Extended mode. The difference is night and day.

📱 Also works on the mobile app.

How to turn it on:
→ Desktop: select Thinking mode, then click the Thinking button in the prompt bar and choose Extended
→ Mobile: tap the thinking mode selector at the top, hit Configure, and set Thinking Effort to Extended

Don't waste this on quick questions. Save it for deep analysis, competitor research, or complex business decisions where quality matters more than speed.
