You Can Now Claim Your Business in ChatGPT

OpenAI recently launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, giving business owners a new way to manage their presence and see how customers discover them.

Here's what you can do:
• Track mentions: See how often ChatGPT references your business website in its answers.
• Measure clicks: Find out how many people click through to your website from ChatGPT.
• Discover topics: See the common themes in ChatGPT answers where your business appears.
• Manage your business: Update your information and connect a product feed with prices and availability.

Here's how to get started:
1️⃣ Go to https://chatgpt.com/business-hub and find your business.
2️⃣ Claim or add your business and complete the required verification.
3️⃣ Once approved, update your details and open Insights to explore your data.

I just went through the process for my own business. In my case, it required domain and email verification, a government ID, and a business registration document.

📌 Good to know: The feature is still in beta, and not every business is eligible. Amazon sellers without their own website may not qualify yet. Claiming your business doesn't guarantee more recommendations.

For the first time, business owners can start measuring how their brand shows up in ChatGPT.
