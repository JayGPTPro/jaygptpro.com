Google just launched "Personal Intelligence" for Gemini

Remember the P.S. from my last post? The update is officially here. Google calls it "Personal Intelligence".

What is the actual update? Until today, Gemini could access your apps (like Drive or Gmail), but it treated them as separate islands. That changes today.

Gemini can now connect the dots between your Gmail, Google Photos, YouTube, and Search history simultaneously.

It evolves from a tool that just "finds files" into an assistant that creates a unified memory of your digital life.

Why does this matter? We all have information scattered across different formats. You no longer need to remember where you saw something - whether it was an email, a screenshot, or a video. Gemini simply sees the full picture and can summarize or retrieve information based on context, not just keywords.

🔒 Privacy Note:
* Opt-in only: It is OFF by default. You must turn it on in Settings > Extensions.
* Private: Your data is NOT used to train their public AI models.
* Control: You can disconnect it anytime.

📍 Availability: It is currently rolling out in the US .

💡 If you were still on the fence about switching to Gemini, this feature might just be the tipping point.
