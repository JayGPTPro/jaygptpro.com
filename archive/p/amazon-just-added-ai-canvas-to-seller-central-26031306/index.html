Amazon just added AI Canvas to Seller Central

Amazon launched a Canvas experience inside Seller Assistant . You ask a question in plain English and get a personalized visual dashboard in seconds.

→ "How are my products performing?" gives you charts with sales, traffic, and trends
→ "What if I raise my price 10%?" simulates the impact in real time
→ "What products should I restock?" gives you recommendations with revenue and cash flow projections

Ask a follow up and everything updates live. It's a conversation with your data.

Powered by Amazon Nova + Claude AI. Free for all sellers in the US and UK.

Try it: open Seller Assistant inside Seller Central and ask it anything.
