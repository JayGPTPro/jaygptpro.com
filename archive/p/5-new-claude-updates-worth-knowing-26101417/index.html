5 New Claude Updates Worth Knowing

1️⃣ Claude inside Google Sheets, Docs & Slides
Install the Google Workspace add-on, and Claude opens in a sidebar next to your file.

For example, open your Amazon sales and PPC spreadsheet and ask:

"Find products where ad spend increased but sales dropped. Create a new tab with the findings and a chart."

Claude can write formulas, build pivot tables, and edit your actual spreadsheet. It can also rewrite Docs and create Slides that match your existing design.

By default, it asks for approval before making edits. Your existing Claude Skills and connectors work there too.
Beta, paid plans.

Also worth knowing: Claude's own Docs, Slides, and Design tools inside Artifacts are now available on the Free plan too. These are separate from the Google Workspace integration.

2️⃣ Claude Dashboards: Build dashboards by asking
Connect a data source like BigQuery, Snowflake, or Salesforce, and describe what you want to track.

Claude creates the charts, writes the underlying queries, and keeps the dashboard updated as your data changes. You can even inspect the queries to see exactly how the numbers were calculated.
Beta, paid plans.

3️⃣ Claude Motion: Turn reports into animated videos
Imagine turning a sales report into a short animated presentation. Charts grow on screen, numbers change, and key findings appear one after another.

Claude creates the animation, lets you edit the text, colors, and timing, and exports it as an MP4.

Think animated charts and motion graphics, rather than realistic AI videos like Kling or Veo.
Beta, Team and Enterprise only.

4️⃣ Haiku 5.5: Faster and cheaper
Anthropic released its fastest and cheapest small model yet, designed for repetitive, high-volume tasks like analyzing thousands of customer reviews, categorizing support tickets, or running AI subagents.

According to Anthropic, it costs around 75% less on average to run than Haiku 4.5.

5️⃣ Free monthly API credits
Claude Max and Team subscribers can now claim monthly API credits:
• Max 5x: $100/month
• Max 20x: $200/month
• Team: Up to $500/month, pooled

These credits work with Haiku 5.5 too, letting you process more tasks with the same budget when that model fits the job.

To claim, link your Claude Console organization under Settings → Billing → API credits.
Important: Pro isn't included. Credits expire monthly and don't increase your Claude Code subscription limits.
