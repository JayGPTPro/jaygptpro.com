This is probably one of the coolest Claude updates we’ve seen in a while

Anthropic just launched a new feature called Record a Skill, which lets you teach Claude a workflow simply by recording your screen.

Instead of writing a long prompt with dozens of steps, you perform the task once, explain out loud what you’re doing and why, and Claude turns the process into a reusable Skill that you can run again and again.

Claude uses the recording and your explanations to understand the workflow, its purpose, and the rules it should follow when working with new files or different situations.

I tested it myself today, and it’s honestly impressive.

Claude didn’t just build the Skill. It understood the goal of the task and even suggested a better and more efficient way to do it than the way I had demonstrated.

How does it work?

1️⃣ Open the Claude Desktop app and go to Cowork
2️⃣ Click the plus icon and select Record a Skill
3️⃣ Choose your microphone and start recording
4️⃣ Complete the task from beginning to end while explaining what you’re doing, why you’re doing it, and what the final result should look like
5️⃣ Stop the recording, and Claude will analyze the process, ask follow-up questions if needed, and turn it into a reusable Skill
6️⃣ Save the Skill and test it on a new example to see what it actually learned

A few important tips before you start:

• Prepare all the files, websites, and windows you’ll need in advance
• Start by explaining the goal of the task and the result you expect
• Don’t just say, “Now I’m clicking here.” Explain why you’re taking each action
• Mention any rules, exceptions, and situations that require a decision
• When moving between files or systems, explain why you’re switching and what you’re looking for
• Stay focused and avoid unrelated actions during the recording
• Don’t expose passwords, private conversations, or sensitive information
• Don’t expect it to be perfect on the first try. Run it, give feedback, and keep improving it

Until now, we mostly taught Claude through written instructions.

Now you can train it the same way you would onboard a new employee.
