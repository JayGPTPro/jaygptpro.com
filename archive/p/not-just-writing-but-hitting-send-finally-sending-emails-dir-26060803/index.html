Not just writing, but hitting Send: Finally sending emails directly from ChatGPT

A small update that is going to save you a ton of time.

How did it work until today?
Open Gmail → copy the email from the supplier → paste in the chat → ask for a draft → copy back to Gmail → edit → send.

How does it work now?
You connect your Gmail account to ChatGPT, and two things happen:
1. Automatic context understanding: The AI is connected to your inbox, so it reads the previous correspondence on its own and drafts a personalized reply. Without you having to explain anything from scratch.
2. Draft it. Tweak it. Send it: The email opens inside the chat in a dedicated built-in editor. You make specific tweaks and hit Send to the recipient.

💡 A small tip from me: To make sure the built-in email editor actually opens inside the chat, start your prompt specifically with the word "Draft" (e.g., "Draft an email to...").

Basically, the distance between asking for something and actually doing it just disappeared.

The catch: You need to consider your commercial data security (how comfortable are you with OpenAI reading your emails?).
