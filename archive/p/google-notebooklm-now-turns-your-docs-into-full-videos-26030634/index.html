Google NotebookLM now turns your docs into full videos!

Remember when NotebookLM launched Audio Overviews and everyone lost their minds over AI podcasts? This is that moment again, but for video.

NotebookLM just dropped Cinematic Video Overviews. You upload any document or spreadsheet, and it generates a polished video with narration and custom animations in just one click.

Google stacked their best tech for this: Gemini 3 acts as the director, Nano Banana Pro handles images, and Veo 3 generates the video footage.

Here are two ways you can use this right now:
✅ Product explainers. Upload your listing bullets, backend keywords, and the listing image folder to get a ready to publish video.
✅ Team training. Upload your text SOPs and instantly get an engaging training video for your staff.

The catch? It is only for Google AI Ultra subscribers and capped at 20 videos a day. But the future of content is officially here.
