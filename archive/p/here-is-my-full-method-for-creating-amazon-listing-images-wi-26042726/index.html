A few minutes ago I gave a talk at AMAZING DAYS · Sofia, Bulgaria 🇧🇬

This is the workflow I shared on stage, and I wanted to share it here with all of you as well.

What makes this workflow so powerful is that the entire process happens inside ChatGPT:

🧠 Product analysis
🎯 Customer psychology
🎨 Visual strategy
✍️ Image prompts
🖼️ Final listing images

From the first planning step to the final image output, everything happens in one place.

The core idea is simple:

The prompt is not the magic.
The magic is what happens before the prompt.

Before generating any image, ChatGPT first builds the thinking behind the image:
* Product strategy
* Customer pain points
* Brand visual language
* A clear role for each image in the listing

Only after that, it creates the prompts and the images.
