This next tip is one of those insights I wish I had understood much earlier

When you think about where to bring AI into your business, do not start with:

“What is the coolest thing I can build with AI?”

Start with:

“What is the bottleneck slowing my business down right now?”

Those two questions lead to completely different outcomes.

You can build something impressive that saves an hour on a task, while the business continues moving at exactly the same speed.

Or you can solve a task that takes ten minutes, but that everything else has been waiting on for three days, and suddenly unlock an entire process.

That is the difference between saving time on a task and actually moving the needle in your business.

So before building another agent, report, or automation, look for the point where the work stops moving.

❓ Where does everything wait?
❓ What keeps coming back for revisions?
❓ Which step still depends on you before anything else can continue?

That is usually where AI can create the most meaningful impact.

Not by building the coolest demo.

By opening the bottleneck that is holding everything else back.

And it may solve one of the biggest bottlenecks in your business today.
