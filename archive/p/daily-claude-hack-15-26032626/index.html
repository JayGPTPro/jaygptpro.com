Daily Claude Hack #15

🤯 Control your computer from your phone. Through Claude.

Anthropic just dropped maybe the biggest Claude update yet: full control of your computer, straight from your phone.

Two new features working together:
→ Computer Use: Claude sees your screen, moves the mouse, types, opens apps. Exactly like a person sitting at your desk.
→ Dispatch: Send a task from your phone, Claude executes it on your computer. Even when you're nowhere near it.

Picture this: you're at dinner. You remember a report needs to go out. You open Claude on your phone, type "open the weekly report spreadsheet, update the numbers, and email it to the team." Go back to eating. Report sent.

How to set it up:
1️⃣ Claude Desktop on your Mac (Cowork mode)
2️⃣ Settings → Computer Use → toggle on
3️⃣ Grant Accessibility + Screen Recording permissions in macOS
4️⃣ On your phone, open Claude and go to the Dispatch tab
5️⃣ Send a task. Watch your computer execute it in real time.

Good to know:
→ Requires a Max subscription (not Pro)
→ macOS only for now
→ Still in Research Preview. Sometimes slow, sometimes confused. Start with small tasks.
→ Your computer needs to be on and unlocked.

The moment you see Claude open a window, type, and hit Send while you're watching from your phone, you realize something has changed for good.
