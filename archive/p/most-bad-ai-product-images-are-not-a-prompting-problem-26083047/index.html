Most bad AI product images are not a prompting problem

I see this all the time. Someone gets an AI image that looks close to their product, but something is off, so they switch models, rewrite the prompt, and keep generating.

But often, the real problem is simpler:
The AI did not get enough information about the actual product.

Reference photos are not marketing photos.
They are information.

They do not need to be beautiful or shot in a studio. An iPhone photo on a table is completely fine.

The goal is simple:
Do not make the AI guess.

When I prepare product references, I focus on four things:
📸 Multiple angles so the AI understands the full shape
🔄 Different states if the product opens, folds, connects, or changes
📏 Real world scale using a hand or a familiar object
🔍 Important details like buttons, stitching, textures, materials, and mechanisms

A compact umbrella is a perfect example. One photo of it closed does not tell the AI how it looks open, how big it is, what the inside looks like, or how the mechanism is built.

For many products, 6 to 10 strong reference images is a great starting point.
Not 10 versions of the same angle.
10 images that each add new information.

The more clearly you show the real product, the less the AI has to invent.

And less invention means much more accurate product images.
