Daily Claude Hack #14

🎯 Three brains, one Claude. Pick the right one for the job.

You don't have to use the same model for the entire conversation.

Type /model and pick:
→ Opus. the smartest. For complex problems.
→ Sonnet. the sweet spot. Fast and smart.
→ Haiku. the fastest and cheapest. For simple stuff.

The smart strategy:
1️⃣ Start with Sonnet for regular work
2️⃣ Switch to Opus when you hit a hard problem
3️⃣ Switch to Haiku for formatting or repetitive tasks

You wouldn't drive a Ferrari to the grocery store. Use the right model for the right task.

On claude.ai: click the model name at the top.
In Claude Code: type /model. No restart needed.

⚠️ Available on Pro, Max, and Team plans.

React with 👍 if you didn't know you could switch models mid chat.
