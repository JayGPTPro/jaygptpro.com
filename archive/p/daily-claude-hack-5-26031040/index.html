Daily Claude Hack #5

🔄 Record your workflow once. Claude repeats it forever.

Remember yesterday's hack about Claude in Chrome? Today is the best part.

Inside the extension there's a feature called Record Workflow. You hit record, do your thing in the browser, and Claude watches everything. When you stop, it doesn't just memorize your clicks. It understands the whole process.

Here's a real example. Say you want to track new competitors entering your niche:

You hit record and Claude watches you:
→ Search your product on Amazon
→ Filter by "Newest Arrivals"
→ Set a price range
→ Go through the results and flag the relevant ones
→ Save them to a tracking spreadsheet or add them to a competitor PPC campaign

You do it once. Claude learns the whole flow. Next time you just type / and select the shortcut. Done.

🤯 And here's the crazy part. You can schedule it. Toggle on "Schedule" in the shortcut settings, pick a time and frequency, and Claude runs it on its own. Every morning. Every Monday. Whatever you need.

You're basically building your own AI employee that works on a schedule.
