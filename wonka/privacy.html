Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This policy describes how JAY GPT PRO LLC ("we", "us") handles your personal information when you purchase or participate in The Wonka Creative Bootcamp (the "Program").

1. What we collect

  • Purchase details: your name and email address when you buy the Program. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
  • Portal access: the member portal signs you in with Google OAuth using your email address, which we check against the round's participant list.
  • Progress data: which days and missions you mark complete, stored so your progress follows you between devices.
  • Support and community: emails you send us, and, if you join the group chat or calls, whatever you choose to share there.

2. What we do with it

We use your information to deliver the Program: portal access, support, Program emails (day unlocks, round logistics), and guarantee handling. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

3. Video watermarking

Program videos display a rotating watermark of the viewing account's email address. This is an anti-piracy measure; it means your email is visible on your own screen while you watch.

4. Service providers

We use a small set of processors to run the Program, each receiving only what it needs: Stripe (payments), Supabase (authentication and progress data), Google (sign-in), Vimeo (video hosting), our email delivery provider (Program emails), and WhatsApp (optional community, only if you join). Your own use of Claude, OpenAI, and Genrupt in the Program happens on your accounts, under their privacy policies, and is not visible to us.

5. Retention and your rights

We keep your data for as long as you have Program access, plus what bookkeeping law requires. You can ask us at any time to correct your data, export it, or delete what we are not legally required to keep. Email info@jaygptpro.com and we will handle it within 30 days.

6. Cookies and analytics

The sales page and portal use only what is needed to function (session state, sign-in). We do not run advertising trackers on the portal.

7. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: info@jaygptpro.com.
