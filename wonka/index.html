The hard truth

AI can make a beautiful image.
It cannot make your package.

Anyone can generate a pretty picture. Sellers fail at the parts around the picture. The factory exists for exactly those parts.

Failure 1

The product is wrong

Generic AI redraws your product from imagination: wrong bottle, wrong size, wrong label. Wonka locks your REAL product as a reference and smoke-tests the lock before any batch.

Failure 2

Nobody inspects

Tiny unreadable text, icon soup, non-compliant badges. Every factory image passes hard gates: a mobile squint test, one message per image, compliance checks.

Failure 3

Nobody tests

Most sellers upload their favorite and hope. Your images race the live listing in the Tasting Room, two races in one sitting, before a single ad dollar.
