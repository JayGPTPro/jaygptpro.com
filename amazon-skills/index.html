amz-buy-boxThe Five Buy Box Gates
Sales dropped 70%. Traffic is flat, I didn't touch my price, and the Buy Box is gone.
1 · EligibilityAccount in good standingPass
2 · FulfillmentFBAPass
3 · Price to customerA similar product dropped, so your price is now above Amazon's fair-price thresholdFail
4 · Account healthStopped
5 · Stock and reliabilityStop at the first failed gateStopped
Fix first: price suppression. Lower to a reasonable price or fix the reference-price flag. The Buy Box typically returns within 24 hours.

Here's my search term report for a stainless steel dog bowl. Find the waste.

"dog water bowl"Converting at $4 per saleMove to exact
"bowl"2 clicks, not enough dataWait
